# 🏃 Agile Scrum Methodology
### Stationery Management & Sales System (SMSS)

---

## 1. Methodology Overview

SMSS adopts the **Scrum framework** — an Agile methodology built on short, time-boxed iterations called **Sprints** — to deliver a working, testable mobile application incrementally.

### Why Scrum for SMSS?

| Principle | How It Applies to SMSS |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Iterative delivery** | Ship working features every 2 weeks, not a big-bang release after months |
| **Early feedback** | Owner/stakeholder tests features early; catches issues before they compound |
| **Flexibility** | Requirements from handwritten notes are still evolving; Scrum embraces change |
| **Transparency** | Sprint boards make progress visible to the owner/sponsor at all times |
| **Risk reduction** | Small increments mean bugs are caught early, not at the end |

---

## 2. Scrum Team Roles

| Role | Person(s) | Responsibilities |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Product Owner** | Business Owner / Sponsor | Owns the Product Backlog; prioritises features; accepts/rejects completed stories; represents end-user needs |
| **Scrum Master** | Lead Developer | Facilitates ceremonies; removes blockers; ensures the team follows Scrum; shields team from scope creep |
| **Development Team** | Developer(s) | Designs, codes, tests, and deploys features; self-organising; cross-functional |

> *For a solo/small team: one person may hold multiple roles. The Scrum Master ensures ceremonies still happen.*

---

## 3. Scrum Ceremonies

| Ceremony | When | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|---------|
| **Sprint Planning** | Day 1 of Sprint | 1–2 hours | Select backlog items for the Sprint; break into tasks; estimate effort |
| **Daily Standup** | Every morning | 15 minutes | What did I do yesterday? What will I do today? Any blockers? |
| **Sprint Review** | Last day of Sprint | 1 hour | Demo completed features to Product Owner; get feedback |
| **Sprint Retrospective** | After Sprint Review | 45 minutes | What went well? What to improve? Action items for next Sprint |
| **Backlog Refinement** | Mid-Sprint (weekly) | 45 minutes | Clarify, estimate, and prioritise upcoming backlog items |

---

## 4. Sprint Configuration

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Sprint Length** | 2 weeks |
| **Estimation Unit** | Story Points (Fibonacci: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) |
| **Velocity Target** | ~20–30 story points per sprint (adjust after Sprint 1 actuals) |
| **Sprint Start Day** | Monday |
| **Sprint End Day** | Friday (end of week 2) |
| **Working Days** | 5 days/week |
| **Definition of Done** | See `04_DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md` |

## 5. Story Point Reference

| Story Points | Effort/Complexity |
|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Trivial — less than 1 hour (e.g. update a label, fix typo) |
| 2 | Simple — 1–3 hours (e.g. add a form field, update a query) |
| 3 | Small — half a day (e.g. build a simple CRUD screen) |
| 5 | Medium — 1–2 days (e.g. complete feature with API + UI + validation) |
| 8 | Large — 2–4 days (e.g. complex feature with auth, realtime, and tests) |
| 13 | Extra Large — needs breakdown into smaller stories |

## 7. User Story Template

```
As a [role],
I want to [action/feature],
So that [benefit/value].

Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given [context], When [action], Then [expected outcome]
  - Given [context], When [action], Then [expected outcome]

Story Points: [1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 8]
Priority: [Must Have / Should Have / Could Have / Won't Have this Sprint]
```

### Example Stories:

```
Story: Owner can add a new centre
As an owner,
I want to register a new stationery centre with a name, location, and centre number,
So that I can begin assigning workers and tracking that centre's operations.

Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given I am logged in as owner, When I tap "Add Centre", Then I see a form
  - Given I fill in all required fields, When I submit, Then a new centre appears in my list
  - Given I enter a duplicate centre_no, When I submit, Then I see an error message

Story Points: 5
Priority: Must Have
```

---

## 8. Definition of Ready (Before a Story Enters a Sprint)

A story is **Ready** if:
- [ ] It has a clear user story statement
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are defined
- [ ] It is estimated in story points
- [ ] Dependencies are identified
- [ ] UI/UX wireframe exists (if applicable)
- [ ] It fits within one sprint

---

## 9. Release Strategy

```
Sprint 0    → Setup sprint (no releasable feature)
Sprints 1–5 → Development sprints (internal testing builds via EAS Preview)
Sprint 6    → Hardening sprint → first production release to Google Play (internal track)
Post-MVP    → Ongoing sprints for enhancements
```

**Release cadence:** At the end of every 2 sprints, a release candidate is built from `main` and distributed via **Expo EAS** internal testing channel to the Product Owner for sign-off.
